# 🇨🇭 [SwissVote EN](https://tgalimberti.github.io/swissvote-en/)

An open-source, static archive providing English translations of Swiss voting materials.

## The Mission
Direct democracy thrives on informed participation. Currently, official voting materials are only provided in German, French, Italian, and Romansh. This project provides fast, accessible, and responsive English translations of Federal and Cantonal voting matters, designed specifically for English-speaking Swiss citizens, international residents, expats, and global students of direct democracy.

## Current Scope
- **Federal Matters**
- **Cantonal Matters:** Ticino (TI)

## How to Contribute (We need your Canton!)
Please read our [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) guide to see how to contribute new matters in your Canton.
