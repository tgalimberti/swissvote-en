<h2>In brief</h2>
<h1>Amendment to the<br>Civil Service Act</h1>

<div class="grid-2-col">
<div class="side-label">Context</div>
<div>
Those who cannot reconcile military service with their conscience can ask to perform civil service. Initially, the credibility of the conflict of conscience was examined by a special commission. Since 2009, it has been considered proven by the willingness of the interested party to perform civil service for a number of days 1.5 times higher than the remaining days of service in the army.
</div>
</div>

<div class="grid-2-col">
<div class="side-label">The amendment to the law</div>
<div>
For the Federal Council and Parliament, it is necessary that civil service remains the exception. The amendment to the law intends to ensure that fewer people switch to civil service, in particular among those who have already completed a large part of their military service and for whom the switch to civil service entails relatively few additional days of service. The amendment therefore provides that at least 150 days of civil service must be performed in any case. The amendment also subjects the planning of civil servants' assignments to stricter conditions, so as to prevent the latter from being advantaged compared to those who perform military service. A referendum has been requested against the amendment to the law, which is why it is put to a vote.
</div>
</div>

<div style="margin-top: 40px; padding-top: 30px; border-top: 1px solid var(--border-color);">
<div class="grid-2-col">
<div class="side-label">The question that appears on the ballot</div>
<div style="font-size: 1.3em; font-weight: bold; color: var(--swiss-red);">
Do you wish to accept the amendment of 26 September 2025 to the Federal Act on Civilian Substitute Service (Civil Service Act, CSA)?
</div>
</div>

<div class="recommendation-box">
<div class="side-label">Recommendation of the Federal Council and Parliament</div>
<div>
<div class="big-rec">Yes</div>
<p>Military service is the rule and civil service the exception. The Federal Council and Parliament intend to strengthen this principle. It is also a matter of solving the problem of the numerous military personnel who switch to civil service after having completed long periods of service. The amendment therefore provides for measures aimed at preventing civil service from entailing more favorable conditions compared to military service.</p>
<a href="https://admin.ch/modifica-servizio-civile" class="url-link" target="_blank">admin.ch/modifica-servizio-civile</a>
</div>
</div>

<div class="recommendation-box">
<div class="side-label">Recommendation of the referendum committee</div>
<div>
<div class="big-rec" style="color: var(--swiss-red);">No</div>
<p>According to the referendum committee, the amendment to the law will cause a significant reduction in the number of people performing civil service. These people will be missing where they are most needed, which will harm social cohesion without however strengthening the army. The amendment is also only the first step towards the abolition of civil service.</p>
<a href="https://legge-servizio-civile-no.ch" class="url-link" target="_blank">legge-servizio-civile-no.ch</a>

<div class="grid-2-col">
<div class="side-label">Six measures</div>
<div>The amendment to the law provides for six measures to ensure that military service remains the rule and civil service the exception.</div>
</div>

<div class="grid-2-col" style="margin-top: 20px;">
<div class="side-label">Minimum number of service days</div>
<div>
Those who switch to civil service will have to perform at least another 150 days of service, regardless of the number of remaining military service days. This prevents the duration of civil service from being reduced to a few weeks or even a few days because the interested party has already performed many days of service in the army. This measure aims to ensure that the army loses fewer soldiers after having invested in their training.
</div>
</div>

<div style="margin-top: 40px; margin-bottom: 40px; margin-left: 25%;">
<strong>The key measure of the draft against late switches to civil service:</strong><br>

<div class="grid-2-col">
<div class="side-label">Identical conditions also for cadres</div>
<div>
Non-commissioned officers and officers who switch to civil service are currently required to perform only ten percent more service days compared to those remaining in the army. Following the amendment, they too will have to perform a number of service days 1.5 times higher. This measure is aimed at reducing the number of army cadres who switch to civil service.
</div>
</div>

<div class="grid-2-col" style="border-top: 1px solid var(--border-color); padding-top: 20px;">
<div class="side-label">Repercussions</div>
<div>
It is not possible to accurately predict what the impact of the amendment to the law will be on the number of civil servants and on army personnel. It is nevertheless reasonable to assume that a smaller number of people will switch to civil service. Over the long term, the contribution provided by civil servants to society will therefore be scaled back.
</div>
</div>

<div class="info-box">
<div class="info-box-title">Military service, civil service and civil protection</div>
<div class="info-box-content">
<p>Swiss men are obliged to perform military service.</p>
<ul>
<li>Those fit for military service must perform service in the army.</li>
<li>Those who cannot reconcile military service with their conscience can ask to perform civil service, where they are employed primarily in the social or environmental field.</li>
<li>Those who are not fit for military service but are fit for civil protection service serve in civil protection.</li>
<li>Those who cannot perform any of these services are required to pay a substitute tax.</li>
</ul>
</div>
</div>

<div class="footnotes">
<p>1 The amendment to the law concerns almost exclusively men. It affects only women who voluntarily perform service in the army and, at a later stage, can no longer reconcile military service with their conscience.</p>
<p>2 Obligations outside of service include in particular the obligation to notify and that of keeping personal equipment in good condition.</p>

<div class="argument-container">
<h2 style="border:none; margin-bottom: 0;">Arguments</h2>

<!-- Referendum Committee (CON) -->
<div class="arg-title con">Referendum Committee</div>
<p style="font-weight: bold; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 1.1em;">The revision causes a massive reduction in the number of people who perform civil service, without however strengthening the army. Civil servants will thus be missing where they are most needed, to the detriment of social cohesion, the environment and the security of Switzerland. The army and civil service should not be put in competition with each other.</p>

<div class="arg-point">
<div class="arg-point-title">Harmful and useless</div>
<div>
By discouraging civil service, society is harmed. The Federal Council estimates that the number of admissions to civil service will decrease by 40 percent. Civil servants are employed in sectors where there is a personnel shortage: in social and health structures, in hospitals, in social services and in schools, but also in environmental protection activities, in agriculture and in the alpine economy.
</div>
</div>

<div class="arg-point">
<div class="arg-point-title">Dangerous</div>
<div>
The introduction of a new deterrent will mean that overall fewer young people will perform a service in favor of society. In light of the current geopolitical situation, this is all the more irresponsible towards our militia system. To guarantee the security of the Country we in fact also need the civil service.
</div>
</div>

<div class="arg-point" style="margin-top: 30px; border-top: 1px solid var(--border-color); padding-top: 20px;">
<div class="arg-point-title">Recommendation of the referendum committee</div>

<div style="margin-top: 60px; padding-top: 40px; border-top: 2px dashed var(--border-color);">
<!-- Federal Council and Parliament (PRO) -->
<div class="arg-title pro">Federal Council and Parliament</div>
<p style="font-weight: bold; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 1.1em;">Civil service is the exception to the rule that obliges all Swiss men to perform military service. Thanks to the amendment to the law, this principle is applied consistently.</p>

<div class="arg-point">
<div class="arg-point-title">Military service is compulsory</div>
<div>
The Constitution speaks clearly: one is not free to choose between military service and civil service. The latter is in fact a substitute service reserved for people who cannot perform military service for reasons of conscience. The amendment to the law strengthens this principle.
</div>
</div>
